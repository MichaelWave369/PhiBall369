# Classic tables: preservation without accidental infringement

This is practical project policy, not legal advice.

A pinball mechanism or broad gameplay principle (flippers, bumpers, ramps, scoring) is not automatically protected by copyright as a game rule. But exact expressive table artwork, characters, music, scripted audiovisual assets, decorative design, and source code may be copyrighted. Names, logos, and distinctive commercial branding may be protected by trademark. A close physical recreation of a recognizable table can raise design, trade dress, or other rights questions beyond individual assets.

**Allowed by default in this repository**
- Our original table geometry, gameplay rules, artwork, UI and music.
- New tables inspired by broad mechanical ideas and historical design eras, without cloning a distinctive commercial machine.
- Third-party assets with explicit compatible licenses and attribution, or verified public-domain material.

**Require review/permission before adding**
- Exact playfield scans, branded table names/logos, manufacturer artwork and music.
- Identical commercial layouts marketed as a specific named machine.
- Reused ROMs, commercial rule sheets, sample packs, copyrighted characters or recordings.

**Heritage workshop design**
- Attach an optional "rights" metadata block to every imported/published table.
- Distinguish licensed restorations, verified public-domain materials, and original "classic-inspired" tables.
- Never imply that age alone puts a game or its graphics in the public domain.
- Allow private local experimentation but do not ship unauthorized branded copies in official game packs.
